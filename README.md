# 🎯 Project Objectives

The major objectives of this project are:

1. To understand digital advertising campaign performance.
2. To clean and preprocess raw advertising datasets.
3. To analyze customer engagement and user-action data.
4. To identify factors affecting conversion rates.
5. To calculate and evaluate important marketing KPIs.
6. To compare campaign and advertisement performance.
7. To visualize marketing trends using analytical charts.
8. To identify underperforming campaigns and content.
9. To provide data-driven recommendations for campaign improvement.
10. To develop reusable analytical metrics for future marketing decisions.

---

# 📊 Key Performance Indicators

The project evaluates several important digital marketing KPIs.

| KPI                 | Description                                                                          |
| ------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **CTR**             | Click-Through Rate – measures the percentage of users who click an advertisement     |
| **CPC**             | Cost Per Click – measures the cost associated with each click                        |
| **CPA**             | Cost Per Acquisition – measures the cost of acquiring a customer/conversion          |
| **ROAS**            | Return on Ad Spend – evaluates revenue generated relative to advertising expenditure |
| **Conversion Rate** | Measures the percentage of users who complete a desired action                       |
| **Engagement**      | Measures user interaction and response to advertising content                        |

# 🔬 Project Methodology

### Step 1 — Data Ingestion

Raw advertising and campaign records are collected and examined for structural inconsistencies.

### Step 2 — Data Cleaning

The dataset is processed to handle missing information, formatting issues, and irregular values.

### Step 3 — Data Transformation

Relevant campaign and user-action information is organized into suitable analytical structures.

### Step 4 — Exploratory Analysis

Campaign groups, advertisement content, and user actions are compared to identify performance patterns.

### Step 5 — KPI Analysis

Important marketing indicators such as CTR, CPC, CPA, and ROAS are evaluated.

### Step 6 — Visualization

Charts and analytical summaries are used to make campaign trends easier to understand.

### Step 7 — Strategic Recommendations

The resulting insights are translated into practical recommendations for campaign optimization.
